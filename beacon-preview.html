Getting started

Beacon brings your education community together. Here's how to begin.

Creating your account

  1. Choose Create an Account on the Beacon home page.
  2. Enter your details and follow the instructions shown on screen.
  3. Return to Beacon to sign in once your account is ready.

Already have a profile?

  1. Select Sign in and enter your first name and surname.
  2. Choose your profile from the matching results.
  3. Confirm your profile and enter your Beacon password. If your profile was imported from EduConnect, verify ownership to activate it first.

What can you do with Beacon?

Beacon is being built to connect education organisations and their staff through professional profiles, career opportunities, organisation tools and shared resources. Features will become available as development continues.
